Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

On this page

    This policy explains what the Discord bot Muninn, developed by Odin, collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

    1. What the bot reads

    The bot can see messages in channels it has access to, but it only processes message content when:

    • you mention it, reply to it, DM it, or use a command;
    • you write in a channel or thread where a server admin turned on auto-reply, or in a running game (for example word chain, counting, story, 20 Questions or a mystery thread);
    • a server admin enabled automod or AI moderation, which checks new messages in that server.

    Attachments you send to the AI (images, PDFs, audio, files) are downloaded temporarily and not stored; only a note of the file name is kept in the chat memory. Messages checked by automod are not stored unless they are posted to the server's log channel by the bot.

    3. Who else receives data

    • Google (Gemini API): when you use AI features, your message text, attached files and recent chat history are sent to Google to generate a reply. When AI moderation is on, messages are sent to Google to be checked. Google processes this under the Gemini API Terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products, and it may be reviewed by humans. Don't send personal or sensitive information to the AI.
    • Server moderators: if a server has a log channel, the bot posts deleted and edited messages, joins and leaves, and moderation actions there.
    • Public lookup services: /weather sends the city name to Open-Meteo, /define sends the word to dictionaryapi.dev, and /wiki sends your search to Wikipedia.
    • Discord receives everything the bot posts, under Discord's Privacy Policy.

    4. Where it's stored

    Data is stored in a database on a server hosted by Google Cloud in the United States. Access is limited to the developer.

    5. Your choices

    • Use /reset to clear the AI's memory of a channel, and /birthday remove to delete your birthday.
    • To delete all your data, or all data for a server you own, email your-email@example.com with your Discord user ID (or server ID). We'll delete it within 30 days.
    • Server admins can turn off features like AI moderation, auto-reply and logging with /config, or remove the bot.

    6. Children

    The bot is not meant for anyone under Discord's minimum age, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

    7. Changes

    We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

    Email your-email@example.com. See also our Terms of Service.
