Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

On this page

    This policy explains what the Discord bot Muninn, developed by Odin, collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

    1. What the bot reads

    The bot can see messages in channels it has access to, but it only processes message content when:

    • you mention it, reply to it, DM it, or use a command;
    • you write in a channel or thread where a server admin turned on auto-reply, or in a running game (for example word chain, counting, story, 20 Questions or a mystery thread);
    • a server admin enabled automod or AI moderation, which checks new messages in that server;
    • a server admin enabled scheduled digests in a public text channel. Digests process up to the latest 100 messages since the prior run, up to seven days, and post only into that same channel. Private channels and threads are excluded.

    Attachments you send to the AI (images, PDFs, audio, files) are downloaded temporarily and not stored; only a note of the file name is kept in the chat memory. Messages checked by automod are not stored unless they are posted to the server's log channel by the bot.

    3. Who else receives data

    • Google (Gemini API): when you use AI features, your message text, attached files and recent chat history are sent to Google to generate a reply. Study-card generation sends the notes or files you choose. Scheduled digests send selected public-channel text and message links to Google, then post a summary in that same channel. When AI moderation is on, messages are sent to Google to be checked. Feedback sent with /feedback, including screenshots, is sent to Google to filter out junk before it reaches the developer. Google processes this under the Gemini API Terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products, and it may be reviewed by humans. Don't send personal or sensitive information to the AI.
    • People in other servers: global leaderboards (/leaderboard scope: Global) show your Discord display name next to your coins, total XP, minigame wins or best chess rating to users of any server with the bot.
    • Server moderators: if a server has a log channel, the bot posts deleted and edited messages, joins and leaves, and moderation actions there.
    • Public lookup services: /tools weather sends the city name to Open-Meteo, /tools define sends the word to dictionaryapi.dev, and /tools wiki sends your search to Wikipedia.
    • GIF providers: when enabled, reaction GIFs and /ikun searches send fixed search phrases to KLIPY or GIPHY, plus the bot operator’s API authentication. Discord user IDs are not sent as GIF-search identifiers. Loading a displayed GIF may contact its media host.
    • NYT: the optional daily Wordle source requests robots.txt and the current date’s public puzzle from nytimes.com. It sends no Discord user IDs, guesses or scores. If access is disallowed or unavailable, the bot uses a local daily puzzle.
    • Discord receives everything the bot posts, under Discord's Privacy Policy.

    4. Where it's stored

    Data is stored in the bot operator’s SQLite database and rotating backups. Access is limited to the developer. Automated expiry runs while the bot is online; expired records may remain until the next cleanup.

    5. Your choices

    • Use /ai reset to clear the AI's memory of a channel, and /birthday remove to delete your birthday.
    • /privacy export privately downloads your attributable bot-database records. /privacy delete asks for confirmation, then deletes personal records and pauses collection until you explicitly use /privacy resume. Deletion removes coins, progress, cards, bookmarks and subscriptions; hosted tournaments are cancelled and current matches forfeited.
    • Self-service deletion excludes staff moderation history, public Discord messages, legacy chat history without author IDs, third-party copies and existing backups. Historical tournament and ticket references are anonymized. Server moderation and audit logging continue under staff settings. Pseudonymized financial cooldowns, claimed quest markers and AI-limit counters remain for up to seven days to prevent reset abuse. An ID and paused preference remain to honor your choice, and anonymous server AI totals remain for quotas. Backups keep the newest seven copies as new backups run; they are not erased immediately. Contact the developer in Discord for remaining requests, including server-wide deletion or legacy records.
    • Unsubscribe from events with /event unsubscribe, leave queues with /queue leave, delete decks with /study delete and remove bookmarks with /bookmark remove.
    • Server admins can turn off features like AI moderation, auto-reply and logging with /config, or remove the bot.

    6. Children

    The bot is not meant for anyone under Discord's minimum age, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

    7. Changes

    We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

    8. Contact

    Contact the developer in Discord or use /feedback in the bot. Do not post personal data or credentials in public support channels. See also our Terms of Service.
