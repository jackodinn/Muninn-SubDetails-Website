Features

Everything your community needs

Type /help in Discord to browse every command by category.

🤖

AI chat

Mention or DM it to chat. It remembers the conversation and reads images, PDFs and voice messages. Give it a persona with /persona.

🧠

AI tools

/summarize long chats, /translate, /search the web with sources, /review code and transcribe voice messages.

🎮

Minigames

Wordle, hangman, trivia, murder mysteries, AI RPGs, impostor, debates, Connect Four, 2048, blackjack and more.

♟️

Chess

Challenge friends in rated games, or the Stockfish bot from Easy (~250) to Grandmaster (~2600). Move history and PGN export included.

📈

Levels & economy

XP and level roles, daily coins, a shop, achievements, giveaways and a /profile card with all your stats.

🛡️

Moderation

Automod, optional AI moderation, logs, warnings, timeouts, verification, tickets and reaction roles.

Games

Real game boards, right in Discord

Boards are drawn as pictures on every move, so games look like the real thing.
